# Espresso Flavoured

*A shot of espresso for your survival world: less stress, less grind, more building.*

Espresso Flavoured is a balanced, cosy take on Minecraft survival for builders. Coffee is the flavour in the name (the way it helps us feel energised and productive), not something you craft in game. Everything here is original, and it runs well on an ordinary PC with a light shader.

It has two parts: the **Espresso Flavoured mod**, which holds the pack's own rules, and the **Espresso Flavoured pack**, which wraps the mod in a small set of mods chosen for builders.

## Pillars

**Calm.** Builders should not have to fear for their work or their progress. Mobs stay a real part of the world, but they stop wrecking builds, and death costs a walk back rather than an afternoon.

**Energised.** Less grind between deciding to build and building: storage that keeps up with the materials builders carry, and quality-of-life that saves time.

**Accessible.** A wider variety of biomes and building blocks within reach, and the tools to find them.

**Light.** Smooth on average PCs, with a shader that looks lovely without a gaming rig.

## The Espresso Flavoured mod (version 0.1)

- **Creepers spare your builds.** Creeper explosions still hurt players and mobs, but they no longer break blocks.
- **Endermen leave blocks alone.** They no longer pick up or move blocks.
- **No phantoms.** Sleeping stays optional.
- **Safe home radius.** Hostile mobs do not spawn naturally within 32 blocks of a bed a player has slept in. Your base becomes a real refuge; the rest of the world keeps its danger.
- **Shulker boxes in the Overworld.** A mid-game recipe (a chest with ender pearls, amethyst shards and iron) so builders don't have to reach the End for storage. The vanilla recipe still works.
- **Easier bundles.** Bundles become reachable earlier, for small items and mixed blocks.

Each rule can be switched off in the mod's settings.

## The Espresso Flavoured pack

- **Graves:** Universal Graves keeps your items safe where you died.
- **Biomes:** Terralith (with Lithostitched) for more variety and more building blocks closer to spawn.
- **Finding things:** Nature's Compass and Explorer's Compass, plus a map (Xaero's World Map and Minimap).
- **Decoration:** Beautify and Rechiseled.
- **Quality of life:** inventory sorting, Mouse Tweaks, recipe and block info (JEI, Jade), searchable settings and controls.
- **Performance:** Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, ModernFix, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling and Dynamic FPS.
- **Shader:** a light shader (MakeUp UltraFast) ready to switch on.

All of these have Minecraft 26.3 Fabric builds (checked 10 October 2026).

## Later ideas

Quick-stack to nearby chests, if a light 26.3 mod appears. More decoration once Adorn and similar mods reach 26.3. A server-friendly version of the safe home radius.

## Principles

Balanced, not easy: the world keeps its danger, it just stops punishing builders. Cosy: rewards making a home. Light: tested on an average PC. Original: no content from other overhauls, and AI help is disclosed.
